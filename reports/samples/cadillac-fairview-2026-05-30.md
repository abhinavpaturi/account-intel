## 4. Pain hypothesis → product pillar
**Pillar:** Real-time AI energy optimization + demand response — bridged to cost + emissions reduction.

Cadillac Fairview is already excellent at *capex* decarbonization — geo-exchange retrofits, heat recovery, zero-carbon certifications. But those projects cut consumption and carbon; they don't touch **Global Adjustment**, which on a large Ontario office/retail portfolio is typically the biggest single line on the electricity bill and is set to climb as 2026 relief phases out. Their HVAC and retail load is exactly the flexible kind that can be curtailed around the Top 5 coincident peaks. Edgecom predicts and curtails those peaks automatically — a software lever (no capital project) that cuts the GA cost *and*, by shifting load to cleaner hours, advances their 35%-by-2030 interim target. It's the operational complement to the capex work they're already known for.
